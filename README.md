## Current Projects

### PepSearch

An evidence-focused platform for peptides and other emerging biological compounds.

PepSearch combines PubMed retrieval, structured LLM-based article analysis, human review, and evidence synthesis to produce transparent, source-traceable summaries of scientific literature.

Current architecture includes:

- PubMed search and article retrieval through NCBI E-utilities
- Alias-aware substance search
- Structured article analysis using OpenAI models
- Primary vs. secondary source classification
- Human review and approval workflow
- SQLite evidence database
- PMID-level provenance and citation tracking
- Automated synthesis of approved evidence
- Planned evidence and safety scoring systems
- Web interface for search, evidence exploration, and literature updates

### APOBEC3A Modeling

Developed a computational model during my internship at New England Biolabs to predict APOBEC3A-mediated cytosine deamination under varying experimental conditions.

The project involved:

- Modeling temperature-, concentration-, and enzyme-dependent reaction behavior
- Parameter fitting and grid-search optimization
- Simulation of thermocycler schedules
- Comparison against experimental methylation-sequencing data
- Python-based scientific computing and data analysis
